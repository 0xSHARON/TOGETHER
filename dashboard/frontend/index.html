Why Choose Together For Your Discord Community

Together brings studio sound engineering to your Discord voice channels. Built on high-performance nodes and optimized sound buffers, the bot eliminates skips, volume distortion, and playback lag during peak server hours.

1. Studio 24-Bit Opus Sound Engine

Together transcodes audio tracks directly into 48,000Hz 24-bit Opus frames. Every frequency from deep sub-bass to crisp treble is preserved, delivering an immersive listening experience for audiophiles and casual listeners alike.

2. Interactive 3D WebGL Webplayer

Transform your listening sessions with a browser-based 3D visualizer. Watch sound frequencies react in real time with dynamic spectrum waveforms, frequency rings, and particle fields while controlling volume, skips, and queues directly from the web.

3. 14 Digital Sound Processing (DSP) Filters

Customize how your tunes sound with 14 built-in digital filters. Switch instantly between Bass Boost, 8D Audio, Nightcore, Vaporwave, Karaoke, Sub Boost, and Treble Boost with simple slash commands.

4. 24/7 Non-Stop Voice Channel Playback

Keep playback going all day and night. Enable 24/7 channel locking to ensure Together stays connected to your voice channel even when everyone leaves, ready to play your favorite tunes when you return.

5. Universal Audio Source Compatibility

Stream tracks, albums, and playlists seamlessly from YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music, and Deezer with automatic link resolving and smart autoplay recommendations.

Complete Discord Bot Command Catalog

Together provides full command parity with 69 prefix commands and 67 Discord slash interactions. Below is a quick reference of everyday commands:

  • /play <query or link>: Play any song, playlist, or album with instant search matching.
  • /queue: View upcoming songs, total playlist duration, and active playback position.
  • /filter <preset>: Apply studio sound effects like 8D, Bassboost, Nightcore, or Vaporwave.
  • /247: Lock the player in your voice channel 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  • /visualizer: Open the real-time 3D WebGL interactive browser visualizer.
  • /vote: Support Together on Top.gg to unlock double listening streaks and exclusive DSP profiles.
  • /forcefix: Instantly reset and reconnect voice buffers if Discord experiences network drops.
